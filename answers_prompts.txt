A MAC address consists of six hexadecimal bytes: Byte Value1	002	133	244	5D5	1C6	5FThe first three bytes (00:13:24) are the manufacturer identifier (OUI), and the last three bytes uniquely identify the network interface assigned by the manufacturer. So if you see either 00:13:24:5D:1C:5F or 00-13-24-5D-1C-5F, they refer to the same network adapter.
